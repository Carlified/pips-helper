Pips Helper reads a screenshot of the day's Pips puzzle and walks you through the next logical step, one hint at a time.

It's a personal project I made for my girlfriend. It isn't a product, it isn't for sale, and it isn't maintained.

Privacy

Everything runs on your device. Screenshots are never uploaded, and there are no analytics, accounts or tracking. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub sees the usual request information (such as your IP address) when the page loads.
